Headline:
  Single-bidder contracts exhibit **+14.8%** higher supply-chain carbon intensity
  (t = 333.7, p < 1e-300, Cohen's d = 0.229).

Sample:
  Contracts: 21,612,129
  Countries: 27
  Years: 2012–2023

RDD (Transparency Threshold):
  - Threshold (EU): €139,000
  - Transparency increases bidders by **+0.77** (absolute) → **+15.19%**
  - Pooled carbon effect near threshold: **−0.635%** (pooled estimate −0.00635 kg CO₂e/USD)
  - 95% CI (pooled): [−0.0117, −0.0010]
  - I² (heterogeneity): **93.3%**

U-Curve (Contract Size Heterogeneity):
  - Small contracts (< €10k): **+50.2%** carbon premium (Cohen's d = 0.75)
  - Mid-size (€10k–€200k): **+12.5%**
  - Large contracts (> €200k): **−7.1%** (reversal)

COVID Natural Experiment:
  - Pre-COVID (2019): ~7% premium
  - During COVID (2020–2021): **20.1%** premium
  - Post-COVID (2022–2023): ~0.3% premium

Mediation through Competition:
  - Proportion mediated: **2.1%** (Sobel p ≈ 0)

Country-level:
  - Significant negative effects: 20 countries
  - Significant positive effects: 5 countries
