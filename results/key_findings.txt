======================================================================
KEY FINDINGS  (current resource)
======================================================================

This repository accompanies the Scientific Data Data Descriptor:

  "Recovered TED bidder counts and full-bid-set eForms tenderers for
   European public procurement."  Ashuraliyev, A. (2026).

It is a DATA RESOURCE, not a hypothesis test. The contribution is the
data and its documentation, not a causal finding.

What the resource provides
--------------------------
Part A  Contract-level competition + carbon dataset, 16,968,922
        de-duplicated contracts across 33 territories (2012-2023):
        8,177,019 TED awards rebuilt directly from the raw contract-
        award notices (eliminating a 2018 ingestion artifact and
        recovering the offers-received count across a schema field-
        rename), plus flagged SECOP (Colombia) and Contracts Finder
        (UK) sources. Observed-bid single-bidder rate ~30.8%, matching
        the official EU benchmark (~29%). Carbon weight validated
        against Eurostat (Spearman rho = 0.82).
Part B  Full-bid-set eForms corpus, 302,555 single-award notices
        (2024-2025), with the complete ranked tenderer set per award.
